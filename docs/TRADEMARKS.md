# The name and the mark

The code in this repository is under the Belay Source-Available License 1.0. `LICENSE` is the whole of what is
granted, and it grants a great deal: copy it, change it, ship it, give it away.
The one thing it does not grant is selling it — see the "NO SELLING" clause in
`LICENSE`. Belay is free, and staying free is deliberate.

What that licence does not grant, because a copyright licence never does, is
the right to use somebody's name for your product. So, plainly:

**"Belay", the Belay wordmark and the Belay app icon are not covered by the
licence.** They belong to PerfectoWeb.

## What that means in practice

**Fork it, rename it, give it away.** Genuinely. Take the code, call it
something else, put your own mark on it, ship it to whoever you like. You need
nothing from us beyond keeping the copyright notice, the credit the licence asks
for, and the one rule that travels with the code: you may not sell it, and
neither may anyone downstream, without written permission from PerfectoWeb.

**Do not ship it as "Belay".** A build that is not ours should not arrive on
somebody's Mac wearing our name, because the person installing it cannot tell
the difference and will come to us when it misbehaves. That is the whole of the
objection, and it is about them rather than about us.

**Say where it came from.** Not a legal requirement, just the ordinary
courtesy: "based on Belay" with a link costs you a line and tells your users
something true.

**Talking about Belay is always fine.** Reviews, comparisons, tutorials,
"works with Belay", a screenshot in a blog post. Nominative use needs no
permission and we are not going to pretend otherwise.

## If you want to use the name anyway

Ask. <https://github.com/PerfectoWeb/Belay/issues> or the address on
<https://perfecto-web.com>. For anything that helps people find the real thing,
the answer is likely yes.
