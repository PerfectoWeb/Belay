Belay 1.6.1

  • A rare overnight crash is fixed. With local reports switched on, the diagnostics log grew heavier with every line until a long night of agent traffic could crash Belay. Found from one precise bug report and fixed for good.
  • Set the timer by end time. The custom timer now takes a length or a clock time — whichever you think in — and shows the other as you type.
  • Take your statistics with you. The erase dialog can export every recorded day to CSV first, and the headline flips to your at-the-keyboard time on hover.

Also: the chart labels bars with real dates on hover, switched-off agent tiles dim as one piece, the Off pill sits back in dark mode, sharing the statistics card now attaches properly in Telegram and friends, and a killed Belay names its own death in the log on the next launch.
