Belay 1.6.1

  • A rare overnight crash is fixed. With local diagnostics enabled, the log could grow heavier over time until a long night of agent activity caused Belay to crash. A detailed bug report helped track it down.
  • Set the timer by duration or end time. The custom timer now accepts either one and shows the other as you type.
  • Take your statistics with you. Before erasing your history, you can export every recorded day to CSV. Hover over the headline to see your time at the keyboard instead.

Also: chart bars now show their date on hover, disabled agent tiles dim consistently, the Off pill looks better in dark mode, shared statistics cards now attach correctly in more apps, and after an unexpected exit Belay records what happened in the log on the next launch.
