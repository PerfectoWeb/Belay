Belay 1.6.4

  • Precise Detection reconnects itself. If the connection to an agent breaks during sleep, Belay restores it when your Mac wakes. No relaunch needed.
  • Long tool calls keep holding. Rapid back-to-back activity no longer interrupts the hold during a long build or test run.
  • One summary when you come back. If Belay held your Mac awake while you were away, you get a single note on return – how long it held and how many runs finished – plus a recent-sessions list under the statistics.
  • A few rough edges are fixed. Statistics no longer flicker on hover, and an Until time that has already passed no longer starts a timer for tomorrow.

Also: watched Cline sessions end when their files disappear, old imported conversations no longer look active, and diagnostics stay fully off when Local Reports is off.
