Belay 1.6.4

  • Precise Detection heals itself. If the link to your agents breaks while the Mac sleeps, Belay rebuilds it on wake and repoints the hooks – no relaunch needed.
  • Long tool calls hold on. Rapid back-to-back tool activity no longer breaks the hold protecting a long build or test run.
  • Small things, straightened. The statistics caption no longer flickers under the cursor, and an Until time that has already passed starts nothing instead of booking a day.

Also: watched-folder Cline sessions end when their files are deleted, imported old conversations no longer appear as working, and diagnostics stay entirely off when Local Reports is off.
