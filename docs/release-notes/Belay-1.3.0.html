Belay 1.3

  • The display dims at night. Step away and it fades to a glow; come back and it brightens. Off by default, in Settings ▸ Behavior.
  • The lid can close now. An optional helper keeps the run alive with the lid shut, and always lets go by itself: at the time limit, or if the Mac gets too warm.
  • Held through the retries. An agent waiting out an overloaded model no longer reads as idle. Belay keeps holding.

Also: small sounds for the welcome and What’s New screens, and a fuller local log. Nothing leaves your Mac, as ever.
