What's New — Belay 1.4.0

The card's text, every language in one place. '# ' starts an item's title, the paragraph under it is the body. Items must stay in the same order in every language. Edit freely, save, then ask for a rebuild: this file gets synced into the code and the string catalogue, and the card shows it.

Direct-only (never shown in the App Store build): none.

=== en ===

# Always On, for as long as you say
Pick fifteen minutes to twelve hours right under the mode picker and watch the time count down. Belay lets go when it reaches zero.

# A pause you can end with one click
When a timer or the maximum awake time stops a hold, the panel says so and offers Hold again. Unlocking your Mac starts a fresh cycle on its own.

# The countdown on the dimmed screen
When the night dimmer takes the display down, the remaining time glows in large thin digits in the middle of it, like a bedside clock.

# The chart plays its outline
Sweep the cursor across the last fourteen days and every bar strikes a note pitched by its height.

=== zh-Hans ===

# 常开，时长由你决定
在模式选择器正下方选择 15 分钟到 12 小时，看着时间倒数。归零时 Belay 会放开 Mac。

# 一次点击即可结束的暂停
计时器或最长唤醒时间停止保持时，面板会说明并提供「继续保持」。解锁 Mac 会自动开始新的一轮。

# 调暗屏幕上的倒计时
夜间调暗器降低显示器亮度时，剩余时间会以纤细的大号数字在屏幕中央发光，就像床头钟。

# 图表会奏出它的轮廓
把光标划过最近十四天，每根柱子都会按自己的高度奏出一个音。
