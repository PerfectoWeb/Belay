Belay 1.6.2

  • Long-running commands keep your Mac awake. Belay now follows silent tests, builds and other tool calls until they actually finish.
  • More sleep-delay options. Hold Shift in the Sleep delay menu to reveal 15 minutes, 30 minutes and 1 hour.
  • Review Precise Detection rules anytime. An agent’s menu now opens Rules… without making you remove detection first.

Also: repeated diagnostics messages now back off instead of flooding the log.
