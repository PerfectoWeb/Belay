Belay 1.4.0

  • Keep your Mac awake for a set time. Choose anywhere from 15 minutes to 12 hours below the mode picker. Belay shows how much time is left and lets your Mac sleep when the timer runs out.
  • Pick up where you left off. If a timer or your awake limit ends a hold, the panel tells you why and gives you a Hold Again button. Unlocking your Mac resumes the hold automatically.
  • See the countdown on the dimmed screen. When Belay dims the display at night, the time remaining appears in the centre of the screen.
  • Hear your week. Move across the chart in Statistics and each day plays a note. Busier days have a higher pitch.

Also: Claude Code and Codex now each have their own switch in Settings. Belay no longer asks for access to folders it will not use. The dimming setting now says when dimming actually happens, What’s New has a new card layout, and the panel no longer moves when you switch modes.
