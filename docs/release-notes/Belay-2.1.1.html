Belay 2.1.1

  • Lid Hold works again after an update. The system kept a stale record of Belay's lid helper after the 2.1.0 update and refused every request to it, so holding through a closed lid silently did nothing. Belay now refreshes the registration at every launch and again if the helper stops answering.
  • Nudge no longer reports a finished session as gone quiet. A /compact after a Stop, a parked session's trailing writes, a session that stopped and expired in the same moment, and a Stop with background tasks past its budget all used to sound as a session that went quiet.
