Belay 1.7.0

  • See what your agents are doing. Working sessions now show small activity badges like shell, editing and search. Categories only, never your prompts or code.
  • Background tasks keep the hold. If agents or shell jobs keep running after a turn ends, Belay keeps your Mac awake and shows a background-task badge.
  • Hooks clean up automatically. Belay removes its entries when it quits and restores them on launch, so closed Belay never leaves agents pointing at a dead connection.
  • Return summaries are more reliable. Closing the lid no longer hides the while-you-were-away summary, and it now mentions if your Mac ran hot.

Also: dark wakes no longer look like stalls in diagnostics, and sessions taking turns no longer flood the log.
